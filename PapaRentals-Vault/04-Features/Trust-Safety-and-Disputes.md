# Trust, Safety and Disputes

## Report vs claim

Two different things read by two different teams, so the labels say so:

- **Report** — the vendor or the gear was wrong. Goes to Trust & Safety.
- **Claim** — something broke and money is owed. Goes to the claims flow.

## Case states  `#decision`

"Reported" covered two situations that feel nothing alike: a form we have read,
and an active arbitration with the vendor's account on the table. Both showed one
red badge and one frozen status, so an open case gave no sign anyone was working
on it — exactly when people start chasing support.

| State | Who holds it | What's expected of you |
|---|---|---|
| `under_review` | Trust & Safety are reading it | Nothing yet |
| `mediation` | Both sides being heard | Evidence welcome |
| `resolved` | Closed | Nothing; decision attached |

The order card follows the same three states rather than showing one red flag
forever. Escalation arrives as a notification — a case that changes hands should
not do it silently.

## Evidence  `#decision`

Mediation explicitly asks for your account of what happened, and there was
nowhere to put it: the one state requiring something from you was the one state
you could do nothing in.

A case now opens to show what you originally reported, where it has reached, and
everything added since. Evidence goes **on the case** rather than into a support
chat nobody reading the case will see. A resolved case keeps its history but
takes nothing new — and says so, rather than offering a field that quietly does
nothing.

## Two-way ratings

Blind: the owner has already rated you, and both publish the moment you submit,
so neither side can retaliate. The renter's half is visible at
[[Profile-and-Identity|/me]].

## Return checklist

On the order detail. Missing accessories are the biggest source of deposit
disputes, and a checklist ticked before handover is cheaper than a claim after.

## Safety copy

- Handover PIN: never share it before the driver is with you — it is what proves
  the gear reached you.
- Vendor threads: keep payments on Papa Rentals; anyone asking you to pay outside
  the app is a scam.
